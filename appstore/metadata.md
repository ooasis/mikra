## Description
Learn to read Biblical Hebrew aloud — ten minutes a day, starting from the alef-bet and building to reading the Book of Jonah in the original.

Mikra is built around one idea: you learn to read a real text, not isolated grammar. Every drill exists to get you reading Jonah.

HOW IT WORKS
• Learn the letters and vowel points with quick spaced-repetition flashcards (SM-2 scheduling)
• One new verse of Jonah each day: listen first with the text hidden, then read aloud, then check the meaning
• Tap any word you don't know — it joins your flashcard drills automatically
• A second wave trails behind: reread each verse cold twelve days later and mark it "owned"
• Hebrew audio for every letter, word, and verse, generated on-device
• Track your streak and your progress across all 48 verses

NO STRINGS
• Works fully offline — no account, no ads, no tracking, no data collection
• Modern Israeli pronunciation
• Designed for complete beginners

TEXT SOURCES
Hebrew text and morphology from the Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible (Westminster Leningrad Codex), CC BY 4.0. English glosses adapted from Strong's lexicon and the World English Bible (public domain).
