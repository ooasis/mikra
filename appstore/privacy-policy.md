# Mikra — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 18, 2026**

Mikra is a Biblical Hebrew learning app for iPhone.

## Data collection

Mikra does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network access.

## Data storage

Your learning progress (flashcard review history, verse progress, streak) is stored only on your device. It is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Third-party services

Mikra uses no third-party services. Hebrew audio is generated on-device by iOS's built-in text-to-speech.
